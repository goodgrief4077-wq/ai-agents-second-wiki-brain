GitHub front page for OpenClaw, an open-source personal AI assistant that runs on your own computer and answers in chat apps such as Discord, Slack and WhatsApp. One Gateway is the control plane; models and agent harnesses are swappable plugins. The README covers install, security notes, governance by the OpenClaw Foundation, and an MIT licence.
