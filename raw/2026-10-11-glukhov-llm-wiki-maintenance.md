Article by Rost Glukhov on keeping an LLM-maintained wiki trustworthy. Its thesis is that such a wiki can look clean while being wrong, so maintenance must check structure and meaning. It names six kinds of drift (source, concept, terminology, decision, citation, structure), proposes a repeating maintenance loop (compile, lint, semantic review, human review of risky changes, commit, scheduled stale-page review), recommends operating files such as a schema and a citation policy, and discusses contradiction handling, review via git diffs and review levels. The page also lists reusable maintenance prompts.
